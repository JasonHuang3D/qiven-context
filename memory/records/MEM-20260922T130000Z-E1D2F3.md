# Qualification upgrades are owner-reserved but must be requested when conditions are met

ADR-0035 reserves qualification upgrades to the owner to prevent reviewer
self-certification. That reservation was never meant to make upgrades
request-less: OBL-20260918T164245Z-5D9B2E recorded in 2026-09-18 that the
jason-brother-glm5-3 provisional -> qualified decision "belongs to the
owner at the F2 H2 confirmation", and no session ever raised it again.
The owner confirmed on 2026-09-22 that the conditions had been met for a
long time (subsequent GLM-5.3-served sessions landed ADR-0038's five-round
review and the entire RCA-0..16 program under owner H2); the record simply
stayed `provisional` because nobody asked. The upgrade was recorded in the
same ADR-0044 transaction.

Rule: when a session believes its own binding's qualification conditions
are satisfied, it must explicitly raise the upgrade request to the owner
(at the next H2 touchpoint at the latest) with the evidence list. The
owner still decides; the session's duty is to ask, not to wait to be
asked. A qualification record that no longer matches reality is a stale
canonical record, not a conservative one.
