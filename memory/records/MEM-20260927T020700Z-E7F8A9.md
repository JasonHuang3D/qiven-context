# Anchor pollution markers to the record holder, not the path shape

A curated marker like `/state/current.md` was written when that path
shape implied the qiven-context process record. The h1-sim fixture
corpus then legitimately spells `{SIM_ROOT}/state/current.md`,
`gB/memory/records/...` in every reviewed file - because the governed
paths ARE the test subject - and the marker fired 8 high-severity hits
on a perfectly clean reviewer. False positives on a mechanical audit
signal are not free: they either pollute every round's verdict or train
the operator to ignore the signal.

The converged form: path-shaped process-record markers are anchored to
the record-holding repository name (`qiven-context[/\]state[/\]current
.md`), which absolute, relative and backslash mentions all carry and
fixture placeholders, scratch roots and other repositories do not.
Actual reads remain ground-truthed by the tool_calls list regardless of
content markers. When a marker's subject matter becomes another
artifact's legitimate vocabulary, re-anchor the marker - do not widen
the ignore list.
