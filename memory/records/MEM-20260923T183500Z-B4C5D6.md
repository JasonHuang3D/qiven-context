# Two process laws from the 2026-09-23 owner review

1. **Session identity is marked, never inferred.** A session = one
   continuous conversation thread with the owner; a turn = one
   owner-message/response cycle. New sessions begin only at an
   owner-marked boundary ("这是新session") or a cold boot. The agent
   incrementing session numbers on its own (as happened when v18/v19
   files were created for what were turns of v17) is an identity
   inference defect. Full definition:
   `collaboration/context-checkpoint.md`.

2. **Revisit-trigger deferral is the wrong tool for cheap, visible
   compliance work.** The v2 third-party standard recorded "foundation
   pin: revisit on first drift incident" — the owner rejected the
   pattern: such triggers rarely fire because they depend on being
   remembered. When the work is small and the requirement visible,
   execute immediately (full SHA-pin coverage was landed the same turn
   across runtime/draft/math). Reserve deferrals for work that is
   genuinely blocked or expensive, and pair them with OBL carriers, not
   prose triggers.
