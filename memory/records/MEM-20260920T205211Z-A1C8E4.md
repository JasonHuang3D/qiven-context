# Pointer-only AGENTS.md; conventions stay in devkit; qiven-runtime activated

Three owner-delegated judgments, decided and executed 2026-09-21:

1. **Conventions stay in the Devkit.** `docs/conventions/` is rolled-out
   engineering law co-located with the templates and adoption machinery
   that stamp every repo; moving it to qiven-context recreates the
   documented 2026-09 failure mode (law invisible to sessions working in
   other repos), and a separate conventions repo adds indirection without
   discoverability. The Devkit self-hosts its own rules.

2. **AGENTS.md is a pointer, not a contract body.** The legacy 119-line
   worker-contract AGENTS.md (~8.5 KB, near-identical across six managed
   repos, mostly superseded ChatGPT Work-mode protocol) is replaced by
   the pointer-only pattern (target <=20 lines: identity, conventions
   index, engineering docs, one canonical-authority line). Canonical
   roles/handoffs/authority live in qiven-context collaboration contracts
   loaded at cold boot; duplicating them per-repo charged every repo
   entry ~8.5 KB of tokens for content no single repo could change
   correctly alone. worker-protocol.md shrank 255 -> 43 lines to a
   mechanical execution-workflow pointer; FULL/FOCUSED stay defined in
   testing-standard.md. Codified as `docs/conventions/agent-entry.md`,
   template 0.1.5.

3. **qiven-runtime activated as the Runtime program home.** The parked
   bootstrap shell (no remote, template 0.1.3, single commit) was
   synced to 0.1.5, pushed to JasonHuang3D/qiven-runtime (public, main),
   and carries the accepted Runtime Component ADL in
   docs/architecture/. The draft repo was deliberately NOT used, to
   avoid semantic drift of the executable-specification repo.
