# Revisit Math scalar vocabulary only when concrete units or numeric integration requires it

Do not broaden `floating_scalar` merely to make the template look more generic. The trigger is a real downstream semantic requirement, especially integration with units or another numeric domain that demonstrates why additional scalar behavior belongs in qiven-math rather than in an adapter or higher layer.
