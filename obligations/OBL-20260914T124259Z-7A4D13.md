# Reassess qiven-context retrieval reliability before resuming Operator merge

The retrieval-reliability gate is complete.

The reassessment froze and exercised open-set retrieval benchmarks across deterministic/metadata, semantic, hybrid/structural, and reranked candidates. The accepted production boundary is deliberately narrower than the experimental branch: retrieval returns source-grounded candidate evidence with provenance; cognition decides answerability and must abstain when canonical evidence does not support an answer. Generic NLI answerability experiments were falsified and were not promoted into the production contract.

Blind-v4 passed the frozen candidate-boundary metrics with required-record recall `1.000`, required-record top-1 rate `1.000`, and zero forbidden top-1 hits. Manual cognition review passed all positive and negative cases. The accepted retrieval production slice was integrated into `main`, including the cold-boot/task-transition CLI boundary and the explicit `untrusted_candidate_evidence / answerability=unresolved` contract.

A fresh two-stage Chat acceptance was then preserved in `evidence/audits/retrieval-reliability-closeout-run001.md`. Stage 1 demonstrated source-disciplined cold boot and task-specific retrieval for the Operator gate; Stage 2 materially switched from Operator/Devkit to CAD, performed fresh CAD retrieval, answered the supported architecture question, and abstained on an unsupported exact IFC-version question. Frozen rubric C1-C10 all passed.

On 2026-09-15 the project owner explicitly accepted the current qiven-context as sufficient to resume Operator work. This satisfies the final completion condition. The retrieval-reliability gate no longer blocks Operator work.

This closeout does not itself authorize Operator merge/release. `OBL-20260914T105500Z-7F31B2` becomes active again and still requires a newly established exact Devkit candidate head, required local regression/dogfood validation, remote review, and ADR-0021 merge discipline before Devkit 0.1.3 rollout.
