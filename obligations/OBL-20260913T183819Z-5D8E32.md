# Establish the CAD provider and headless semantic pipeline before editor-first implementation

The CAD product must earn a deterministic semantic core before UI complexity is allowed to become the organizing architecture. AutoCAD, 3ds Max, UE, or future editors may integrate with the pipeline, but they do not replace the provider/entity/topology/building-model contracts.
