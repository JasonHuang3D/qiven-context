# Validate qiven-context-draft as the executable specification of the cognition architecture

The draft compiles and its loop demo behaves correctly, but per ADR-0037 and
the K4 precedent, a working mechanism is not an accepted mechanism. This
obligation names the path: design the validation profile (which continuity
property the draft must prove, and under which delivery profile), execute it,
bind evidence to the exact draft commit, and only then let draft semantics feed
kernel and K5 design as accepted material. Until then, current context
capabilities remain authoritative and the draft stays quarantined from
production mechanism.
