- **Engineering philosophy:** `collaboration/software-engineering-philosophy.md` and ADR-0024 now define the cross-Qiven implementation posture. Correct downward semantic ownership outranks cosmetic repository independence. When a needed capability naturally belongs to a lower layer, improve that owner first and consume it rather than writing a weaker product-local duplicate and planning extraction later. Multiple consumers are evidence, not a prerequisite for correct placement. Foundation remains strict/product-free; capabilities below a product but above Foundation belong in an appropriate shared layer such as Runtime or Networking. AI-led development should apply mature engineering knowledge directly; experiments and compiler/test loops resolve genuine unknowns and prove behavior rather than deliberately reproducing avoidable human trial-and-refactor history.
- **Engineering doctrine:** A real problem is not automatically the current problem. Discovering a glitch does not by itself justify target switching. Interrupt the mainline only for irreversible damage, a concrete blocker to the immediate next step, or a tiny permanent fix to high-frequency friction. Otherwise observe, record, and continue. This scope discipline does not override correct semantic ownership: a lower-layer change required by the active product is part of the product objective when that dependency is real.
