# Keep the industrial-gas product stack independent from the native C++ core unless domain needs justify coupling

## Context
The industrial-gas management product is primarily an enterprise/business operating system involving customers, personnel, tasks, field activity, workflow, data, auditability, and AI-assisted business processes. Its strongest reuse opportunities with other Qiven products are not necessarily low-level native C++ primitives.

## Decision
Do not require the gas vertical to depend on qiven-foundation, qiven-math, or the native CAD/robotics stack merely for ecosystem uniformity. Choose implementation technologies according to the product's operational requirements.

Share AI gateway patterns, protocol/data contracts, workflow concepts, identity/audit conventions, and other genuinely cross-product services when those boundaries become stable. Native C++ components may be introduced only for concrete performance, device, simulation, or integration needs.

## Alternatives Considered
Forcing every Qiven product onto the same C++ dependency graph; duplicating AI/data/workflow infrastructure separately even when stable cross-product services emerge; treating language uniformity as an architectural goal.

## Consequences
The gas product can optimize for business-system delivery and deployment while still participating in the Qiven ecosystem through explicit service/data boundaries. Cross-product reuse must be justified semantically rather than by branding or language consistency.

## Revisit Conditions
Revisit specific native dependencies when a concrete gas-domain feature requires native compute, hardware integration, simulation, or shared low-level representation with another Qiven product.
