# Adopt single-session unified engineering: role packages become attribution and qualification labels, not behavioral staging

## Context

ADR-0004 separated "architecture and release authority" (jason-brother) from
"local implementation execution" (jason-worker) as two staged roles with
cross-stage handoffs, originally shaped by a ChatGPT-era capability split
(cloud chat agent without local execution + local execution relay). The
devkit engineering templates encode that staging: a Roles section, a
Chat-to-Work-to-Chat normal-flow diagram, "quality expected from
jason-worker" framing, CTO-review escalation points, and a
brother-assigns-to-worker feature-spec contract.

Since then the actual operating topology changed: work is performed by a
single foreground owner-supervised agent session (ZCode under
ContextView<ZCode, Jason>, frequently under the owner-granted
jason-extended-cognition designation) that designs, implements, tests,
validates, and publishes under owner H2 gates in one concurrent session.
No cross-session role handoffs occur; cross-session continuity is already
owned by qiven-context cold boot plus session checkpoints. The staged
partition survives only as residual text that later sessions re-load at
every cold boot and re-apply as behavioral restrictions the owner no
longer intends ("you are worker, so you do not design"; "you are brother,
so you do not run builds").

The jason-extended-cognition designation (PR #33) already suspended the
draft repository's role partition; that suspension proved the unified
model in production sessions (v11, v14, v15 - the entire RCA-0..RCA-16
program landed in single-session mode).

## Decision

1. **Single-session unified engineering is the default execution model**
   for capable agent sessions. One session exercises the full engineering
   loop directly - task specification, design, implementation, testing,
   validation, gating, publication under H2, and the closing context
   transaction - with no role-stage handoffs inside the loop. There is no
   behavioral partition of engineering work by role name: any qualified
   session may design, implement, build, test, measure, review, and
   publish within its authorized scope and the owner's H2 boundaries.

2. **Role packages become labels, not stages.** `jason-brother`,
   `jason-worker`, and owner-granted designations such as
   `jason-extended-cognition` survive as: (a) attribution values in
   commit trailers (provenance, never authority); (b) binding/qualification
   labels in ContextView files per ADR-0035 (evidence-based model
   qualification is unchanged); (c) historical record names. They no
   longer partition behavior, route work, or gate who may perform which
   engineering activity. The routing rule "use jason-worker by default
   for real local execution" is repealed.

3. **What the roles protected is redistributed, not dropped:**
   - Review discipline (formerly jason-brother's) becomes the session's
     own duty: exact-delta self-review before every H2 request;
     semantic acceptance is never equated with a passing CI run; memory-
     delta/context-transaction duty at material changes; rejected
     alternatives and negative knowledge recorded.
   - Publication authority separation (ADR-0004's surviving intent)
     continues through ADR-0036 typed handoffs: H2 owner (or standing
     delegated) acceptance before merge-class publication, governance
     classes owner-reserved, unattended automation read-only by default.
     Authority separates at the owner boundary, not between agent roles.
   - Local-execution honesty and bounded discipline (formerly
     jason-worker's) are already session-level contracts (hang contract,
     evidence honesty, bounded waits, operator-mediated long commands).
   - Deep-reasoning posture and proactive engineering (formerly the
     extended-cognition designation's privileges) become the standard
     expected posture for engineering sessions: maximum available
     reasoning depth; think beyond the literal instruction; propose
     more robust or more maintainable alternatives when they exist;
     proactively flag risks, edge cases and design smells - silence
     about a known problem is a defect.

4. **Devkit engineering templates become role-free engineering-grade
   standards.** The five `templates/cpp-library/managed/docs/engineering/`
   documents are rewritten: the Roles section and staged normal-flow
   diagram are replaced by the single-session engineering loop;
   implementation-standard, testing-standard and feature-spec content is
   preserved (its substance was never role-dependent) with CTO/worker
   framing recast to owner/architecture decision points and
   spec-authorized scoped validation; worker-protocol becomes
   execution-protocol (no cross-session handoff staging); the session
   scar hardening rules (native file-write authoring, git add -N ban,
   merged-state verification, absolute-path landing re-verification, PR
   body quoting discipline, adversarial/concurrency testing posture,
   build-performance measurement discipline, semantic-ownership-first
   placement) are added as standard clauses. Rolled copies propagate to
   qiven-foundation, qiven-math, qiven-runtime and qiven-context-draft
   as managed syncs.

5. **Terminology mapping for prior records.** In accepted ADRs and
   contracts that predate this decision, references to jason-brother/CTO
   decisions map to owner-designated architecture decision points, and
   references to jason-worker execution map to the executing session.
   ADR-0006's FULL/FOCUSED validation substance stands with "explicit
   CTO scope" read as "explicit task-specification scope". This mapping
   clause supersedes wording, not substance; ADR-0035's role/model/
   binding separation and ADR-0036's typed handoffs are untouched.

6. **chatgpt-jason degrades role staging to labels (option B, owner
   adjudication).** The ChatGPT view's human-relay workflow (Human
   Manual Mode) is a capability-class topology, not role staging, and
   stays; its role-partition language is recast to labels. The view's
   stale Workflow-2/current-state wording (referencing the sealed
   qiven-host and the archived OBL-20260915T163500Z-9D4C72 as if
   active) is reconciled to ADR-0043: machine-local governed mutation
   is RuntimeHost-internal (Runtime real-adapter path), and no ChatGPT
   mutation transport is enabled by this decision.

7. **The agents_md_bypass privilege of the extended-cognition view is
   merged into this decision** (its subject - the draft repository's
   role partition - disappears with the template rewrite); the view
   file retains a historical note.

8. **jason-brother-glm5-3 qualification is recorded as qualified**
   (owner statement 2026-09-22: conditions were met long ago; the
   upgrade was never requested). The qualification-upgrade mechanism
   lesson is recorded: upgrades are owner-reserved, but a session that
   believes its binding's conditions are met must raise the upgrade
   request explicitly - silence leaves canonical records stale
   (MEM-20260922T130000Z-E1D2F3).

## Alternatives Considered

De-role only the devkit templates (rejected: templates defer roles to
canonical context; every cold boot would re-load the partition and the
change would be cosmetic); keep the partition for ChatGPT sessions
(rejected by owner adjudication - option B: label degradation applies
there too); retire role names entirely including attribution values
(rejected: attribution provenance and ADR-0035 qualification evidence
remain useful and are not behavioral restrictions); delegate review
authority to sessions wholesale (rejected: H2 publication gates and
governance-class owner reservation are owner-control boundaries, not
role cosplay, and stay).

## Consequences

Cold boots no longer install a two-role behavioral partition. Engineering
standards live role-free in devkit conventions and each repository's
docs/engineering/; canonical collaboration contracts carry authority and
handoff law without personified staging. Commit attribution trailers
(role:/LLM:) remain mandatory and unchanged in format. The supervised
local-agent workflow states publication discipline (formerly "worker
discipline") as a session rule. Nothing in this decision weakens H1-H4,
the no-verbal-waiver protocol, governance-class owner reservation,
unattended read-only default, single-writer discipline, or the
seal/museum policy.

## Revisit Conditions

A participant combination whose tool genuinely cannot execute locally
reappears as the primary work surface (re-evaluate a relay workflow -
capability-class topology already covers this); or evidence shows the
session-internalized review discipline failing to catch a defect class
that independent staged review previously caught (strengthen gates or
re-introduce an independent review role for that class); or the owner
re-introduces deliberate multi-session parallelism (then re-derive
coordination from typed handoffs, not from the retired partition).
