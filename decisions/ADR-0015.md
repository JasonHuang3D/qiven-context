# Keep coordinate conventions and approximate tolerance explicit at higher semantic boundaries

## Context
CAD, scene, robotics, physics, rendering, glTF, USD, Unreal, and MuJoCo may use different coordinate conventions and tolerance meanings. Hiding one global convention or epsilon inside qiven-math would silently impose domain policy on unrelated consumers.

## Decision
qiven-math does not choose a product/world up-axis or handedness. Domain/import/export/scene boundaries own conversions. Vector `operator==` is exact component equality; there is no global epsilon and no approximate equality operator. Approximate APIs must take explicit tolerance, while CAD topology tolerance belongs to geometry/CAD algorithm context rather than a global math constant.

Future matrices use explicit mathematical conventions documented by qiven-math: `M(row,column)` indexing, column vectors, `v' = M * v`, parent/local composition as `Mworld = Mparent * Mlocal`, and standard right-hand-rule cross products. Storage order remains a separate implementation concern.

## Alternatives Considered
Embedding Unreal/CAD/world coordinate policy directly in vector/matrix types; defining one global epsilon; making `operator==` approximate; conflating matrix storage order with mathematical convention.

## Consequences
Callers must state conversions and tolerances where semantics are known. Exact algebraic equality remains simple and deterministic, while domain algorithms can choose tolerances appropriate to scale and topology.

## Revisit Conditions
Revisit only if a universally valid semantics-free tolerance abstraction emerges; product-specific coordinate policy remains outside qiven-math.

## Provenance
Genesis reconstruction from the committed qiven-math architecture and current exact vector equality implementation.
