# Layer the shared native core by semantic responsibility instead of forcing product verticals into one stack

## Context
Qiven spans CAD/3D, industrial-gas management, robotics/simulation, and future physics. Those products share some concepts but do not share one implementation language or one runtime shape. Treating every product as a consumer of the same C++ base would create artificial coupling.

## Decision
The native shared core is split by semantic responsibility. Foundation is the low-level C++ base. Math and units are independent siblings above Foundation; units does not depend on math merely because both participate in geometric calculations. Geometry consumes both math and units when geometric semantics require them.

Product verticals consume only the shared layers that are natural for their domain. CAD and robotics may consume native math/geometry/scene/compute layers. The industrial-gas management product may share protocol, AI, data, workflow, and business concepts with the wider Qiven ecosystem without being forced to depend on the native C++ Foundation layer.

Shared layers must not absorb product-specific semantics simply to increase apparent reuse.

## Alternatives Considered
One universal C++ substrate for every Qiven product; making units a child of math; placing product-specific CAD, robotics, or gas behavior into Foundation/shared math for convenience.

## Consequences
Cross-product reuse remains explicit instead of architectural by default. Some products will legitimately use different implementation stacks. Shared native libraries stay narrower, while product integration occurs through stable semantic, protocol, and data boundaries.

## Revisit Conditions
Revisit a dependency edge only when real consumers demonstrate a stable semantic relationship that is stronger than convenience or temporary implementation reuse.

## Provenance
Genesis reconstruction from the accepted ecosystem discussions, corroborated by the current Foundation and Math architecture documents that already enforce downward dependencies and keep geometry, units, CAD, robotics, physics, and rendering semantics out of qiven-math.
