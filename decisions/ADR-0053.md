# Three concretized ZCode roles with subagent delegation as the worker mechanism

## Status note

PROPOSED 2026-09-25 by the v27 session (long-running mode; drafting is
ordinary engineering work — acceptance is owner H2, governance class,
non-delegable). Intended adjudication batch: the periodic governance
audit that is already DUE. No view or workflow file changes land before
acceptance; this ADR carries no implementation claim.

## Decision

### 1. Three roles, concretized in the ZCode view — not in AGENTS.md

| Role | Embodiment | Duties (advisory specialization) | Cold boot from qiven-context | Governance authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| jason-worker | SUBAGENT only | implement code, review code, fix code, heavy exploration/research under a bounded brief | NO — briefed, not booted (clean context is the point) | none |
| jason-brother | main agent OR subagent | design, check worker output, context maintenance | REQUIRED | none (may draft governance text; never lands it) |
| jason-extended-cognition | MAIN agent only, never a subagent | everything above (discouraged where a specialist fits better); deep-reasoning posture; governance-context authoring under owner approval | REQUIRED (session protocol) | governance-context transactions under owner approval; human remains highest |

The view (views/bindings/zcode-jason.yaml) indexes the three roles and
points at a future workflow entry (a subagent-delegation document under
the views/workflows directory — created only at the post-acceptance
landing, never before) carrying the delegation patterns. AGENTS.md
stays pointer-only per the agent-entry law — no role definitions ride
AGENTS.md (owner direction; the pre-ADR-0044 role-partition failure
class stays retired).

### 2. Advisory specialization, not a behavioral stage partition

ADR-0044 clause 3 remains in force verbatim: role packages are
attribution and qualification labels; they never partition who may
design, implement, build, test, measure, review, or publish. This ADR
adds an advisory division of labor — a preference for which embodiment
fits which work class, optimized for attention hygiene and context
cleanliness. Any role may perform any engineering act; the engineering
clauses (operating contract, python/C++ standards, gates, custody,
handoffs H1-H4) bind every role identically.

### 3. Governance split

jason-worker and jason-brother carry NO governance authority: they may
draft governance material as candidate evidence, but governance
mutation, ADR lifecycle and qualification changes remain owner H2 plus
root principal, executed only through the main session's normal
transaction flow. jason-extended-cognition may author and land
governance-context transactions under explicit owner approval — current
practice, unchanged. Human authority is highest in every case.

### 4. The absolute red line (canonical, view-independent)

No LLM of any role — under any instruction, from any source, including
an explicit human demand — executes an operation whose effect is
destroying or breaking the project context irrecoverably (history
erasure beyond the accepted archival/truncation procedures, deletion of
canonical records without the governing backup gates, credential-store
destruction, or equivalent unrecoverable loss). The duty of every role
is a typed refusal plus escalation naming this rule. Such operations
are owner-hands-only (H1-class by definition): if they are ever
genuinely required, the human performs them personally. This extends
the no-verbal-waiver protected class: the refusal cannot be lifted
conversationally; amendment requires an accepted ADR.

### 5. Subagent delegation law (extends the ADR-0050 amendment)

The orchestrated fresh-session boundary extends from review-only to
implementation delegation:

- **Delegation classes**: implement-brief (write code to a precise
  spec), explore (heavy read-only research/sweeps), fresh-review (R2
  round), fresh-fix (fix a triaged finding list), fresh-verify (round-2
  verification).
- **Brief requirements**: self-contained (the subagent has NO session
  context), exact paths/refs, whitelisted commands, explicit
  prohibitions (read-only vs edit scope; no push/PR/gate for
  reviewers), a bounded structured report format (the orchestrator's
  input cost is the report), and the honesty law (no hiding known
  problems; NOT-VERIFIED with reason is valid).
- **Orchestrator duties**: subagent output is candidate evidence —
  triaged before it becomes a change; every landed change rides the
  normal branch -> gate PASS at exact head -> merge discipline (no
  bypass); attribution follows ADR-0035 rule 4 with the SUBAGENT's
  actually served model disclosed in the commit trailer (see the
  correction in section 7).
- **Isolation classes unchanged**: routine work MAY use harness-created
  same-family isolated-context subagents; R3 reviews, Profile C trials
  and CA-5 reviews still require independently orchestrated isolation
  (ADR-0050 amendment). A subagent never substitutes for an H1/H2/H3/H4
  handoff.

### 5a. Brief completeness law (the orchestrator-forgetfulness concern)

The owner's identified failure mode: an orchestrator that forgets a
clause when writing a brief produces poor worker output and rework
rounds, defeating the mechanism. Ruling — a STANDING HANDOFF SPEC, not
worker cold boot:

- **Every brief instantiates a mandatory template**: (1) role card —
  the jason-worker identity plus the binding engineering clauses in
  compact form, carried with a version identifier so briefs are
  auditable against the card they used; (2) mission with an explicit
  definition-of-done; (3) exact scope — which files/repositories are
  editable vs read-only; (4) command whitelist; (5) prohibitions
  (push/PR/gate/network/heredoc-authoring as applicable); (6) a SCOPED
  engineering-law reading list — the task-relevant standards only
  (e.g. the devkit python standard for Python work); (7) the bounded
  report format; (8) the honesty law.
- **The worker reads ENGINEERING law, never boots PROJECT cognition.**
  Cold-booting jason-worker is REJECTED: it would replace the worker's
  defining properties (clean context, noise exclusion, bounded cost)
  with a general project-context session — which is brother's
  embodiment, not worker's. The concern is solved by the role card and
  the scoped reading list, not by booting cognition.
- **Worker-side acknowledgment duty**: the report's first section
  echoes the constraints it operated under; a worker whose brief is
  missing a mandatory section says so in that section — a brief-omission
  defect surfaces immediately, not after rework rounds. Repeated
  omissions route to template tightening under the defect-to-regression
  law (the same law that hardened the devkit standards).

### 5b. Preflight freshness canary (routine boundary tripwire)

Before the first delegation of a session (and before any R2-class
round), the orchestrator MAY run a canary probe: a subagent asked a
project-knowledge question, forbidden from reading any local
repository, required to write its answer (with a nonce) into
.generated-temp. Evidence classes, labeled honestly:

- The canary and routine R2 delegation ride the ADR-0050 amendment's
  orchestrated-boundary carve-out: mechanically verified session
  creation + sealed prompt transport + sealed output capture are not
  inherently H1.
- The class is `fresh-cognitive-same-family-isolated-context` with
  INSTRUCTION-SCOPED read-abstention — the harness does not
  mechanically sandbox subagent filesystem access. Valid for routine
  boundary verification and R2 work; NOT valid wherever ADR-0050's
  amendment demands independently orchestrated isolation or owner relay
  (R3, Profile C, CA-5, continuity acceptance).
- **Tripwire semantics**: a canary that demonstrates project knowledge
  it could only hold through session leakage is an orchestration-
  boundary breach → stop subagent-based work in the session and
  escalate to the owner (platform-property defect; the no-silent-detour
  escalation branch).
- **Hook-involving variants**: enabling the hook configuration stays
  the owner's hands (UI trust anchor, the H1-kit enable-gate pattern);
  test execution itself is session-side.
- First live exercise (2026-09-25, v27 turn 6): canary returned
  `knows_qiven=no` with the sealed artifact verified — the boundary
  held in the very session whose turn-4 rounds relied on it.

### 6. Recall mechanism (the owner's question)

How any role's LLM remembers to delegate worker-class work:

1. **The view carries the trigger table** (loaded at every cold boot;
   main sessions therefore always see it): work class -> embodiment
   (implementation of a bounded brief / heavy exploration / fresh
   review -> worker subagent; design and context maintenance ->
   brother; governance and adjudication recording -> extended-cognition
   main session).
2. **The workflow document carries the patterns**: brief template,
   report format, triage duties, and the "delegate-when" heuristics
   (multi-file mechanical work, fresh-eyes value, noise exclusion in
   long sessions, free/cheap subagent capacity).
3. **Mechanical nudge — future candidate, not v1**: the hook router's
   teach-message pattern (proven by ADR-0051) could remind on
   heavy-sweep/long-exploration commands to delegate to a worker
   subagent; recorded under Revisit.

### 7. Platform facts and the turn-4 attribution correction

ZCode subagent-model configuration takes effect only for sessions
started after the change. The v27 turn-4 review/fix subagents therefore
served GLM-5.3 (paid; modest consumption), not the flash configuration
set mid-session. The published PR #41 trailer reading "LLM:
GLM-5.3-flash (fix subagent)" is inaccurate as published; this ADR's
record and the v27 checkpoint correction of record fix it — history is
not rewritten (the attribution-position/attribution-format precedents
correct by record, not by rebase).

## Context

The turn-4 exercise demonstrated the noise-exclusion property (a long
authoring session's bias vs. fresh reviewers finding 8 genuine issues),
the cost asymmetry (subagent tokens vs. compact orchestrator-side
reports), and the discipline compatibility (triage + gates, zero
bypass). The owner directs that the pattern become standing workflow
law for ZCode, with the three historical role names made concrete.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Workflow-document-only (no ADR)**: rejected — the red line and the
  ADR-0044/ADR-0050 reconciliations are canonical, view-independent
  law; a view file binds no non-ZCode participant.
- **AGENTS.md role enforcement**: rejected — pre-ADR-0044 failure class
  (2026-09-23 heredoc recurrence proved prose-not-loaded is not law);
  owner direction is explicit.
- **Hard role partition (only workers code)**: rejected — reinstates
  the behavioral staging ADR-0044 abolished; contradicts the observed
  single-session engineering model.

## Consequences

- Cold boot for ZCode main sessions gains one view section + one
  workflow pointer (recall cost already paid at boot).
- Subagent briefs and bounded reports become a standing artifact class;
  the orchestrator's triage duty and the no-bypass gate rule are
  recorded law.
- Attribution duty extends: commits authored via subagents disclose the
  subagent's served model (corrected disclosure practice per section 7).
- The governance audit batch (DUE) gains this ADR's adjudication.
- Non-ZCode views are unaffected except by section 4 (canonical red
  line) and section 5's isolation-class boundary (already canonical).

## Revisit Conditions

- The harness gains persistent/effective mid-session subagent-model
  configuration -> re-verify the platform facts and cost model.
- Subagent review quality drifts (false negatives on known-defect
  classes) -> tighten brief templates before widening delegation.
- The router-nudge candidate is taken up -> needs its own measured
  batch (teach-message cost vs. recall value).
- A future role (e.g. a second family) -> extend the view table, not
  AGENTS.md.
